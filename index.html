FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the trainer, compatibility and safety.

Is the trainer free and open source?
Yes. The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin Trainer is completely free and its source is published openly. You can review the code, report issues, or fork it from the project page at gitscore.cc.
Which Windows versions are supported?
The trainer targets Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It runs as a portable executable, so there is nothing to install — just launch it before or after the game.
Do the god mode and infinite gold toggles work in co-op?
The trainer is designed for single-player and private sessions. Use it offline or in your own hosted matches only — enabling cheats in public or competitive lobbies may violate the game's terms of service.
Will the trainer get my account banned?
Any third-party tool carries risk. Use it on a secondary account, avoid public multiplayer, and always download from the official gitscore.cc page to make sure you get the unmodified build.
